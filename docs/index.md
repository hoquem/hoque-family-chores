# Chores Star — Support

Chores Star is a family chore app. A grown-up sets up a family, anyone can add
chores, and children earn stars for the work they do and spend them on rewards
the family agrees on.

## Common questions

### How does my child join the family?

A grown-up creates the family first, then shares the family's invite code from
the **Family** tab. On the child's device, tap **I'm a kid — join my family** on
the sign-in screen and enter that code. Children do not need an email address
or a password.

### How do stars work?

Every chore is worth a number of stars. When someone finishes a chore they mark
it done, and it waits for another member of the family to confirm it — you
cannot confirm your own work. Once confirmed, the stars are added and can be
spent on rewards in the **Treats** tab.

### Can a child see other families' chores?

No. A family only ever sees its own chores, members and rewards.

### How do I delete my account and data?

Open the **Profile** tab and choose **Delete Account**. This removes your
profile and your personal data. If you would rather we did it for you, email
the address above from the account's email address and we will confirm once it
is done.

### I've forgotten my password

Enter your email address on the sign-in screen, then tap **Forgot Password?**
and we will send you a reset link. If you signed in with Apple or Google, use
that same button rather than a password.

### The app is stuck or won't load

Close it fully and open it again. If it still will not load, check that the
device is online, and email us — tell us what the screen says and we will look
into it.
