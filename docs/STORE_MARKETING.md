# Store Marketing & Metadata — Chores Star

This document contains the official App Store and Google Play Store marketing copy, positioning, and keywords for **Chores Star**, focusing on the **AI Chore Coach**, motivation, and family life-skill learning.

---

## 🎯 Strategic Value Proposition & Marketing Angles

### 1. The Real Family Problem
- **Nagging exhaustion**: Parents hate having to constantly remind, negotiate, and enforce chores.
- **Child overwhelm**: Vague requests ("clean your room") overwhelm kids, leading to avoidance or tears.
- **Lack of meaning**: Chores often feel like pointless drudgery rather than personal mastery or contribution.

### 2. How the AI Chore Coach Solves It
- **Playful Motivation & Pep Talks**: Turns boring chores into energetic mini-missions with playful challenges (racing a 3-minute hype song, stealth ninja mode) so kids jump in excited.
- **Bite-Sized Action Steps**: Breaks every chore down into 3–4 practical, sequential steps suited to children aged 6–14.
- **Teaching the Lifelong "Why"**:
  - **For You**: Fosters personal pride, self-reliance, calm, and ownership of their space.
  - **For Your Family**: Teaches empathy, teamwork, and lifting the household load together.
  - **For Your Home**: Demonstrates how small daily habits create a clean, cozy, peaceful haven.
  - **Superpower Life Skills**: Connects everyday chores to lifelong superpowers (organization, resilience, consistency).
- **Personalized Parent House Notes**: Parents can add specific instructions (supply locations, safety rules) and the AI coach weaves them seamlessly into the guide.

### 3. Sustainable Habit System
- **Real Treats Store**: Stars are exchanged for meaningful family experiences (movie nights, outings, favorite treats, extra screen time).
- **Daily Streaks & Friendly Leaderboard**: Builds daily consistency and visual progress.
- **Before & After Photo Proof**: Kids take pride in their work; parents verify in seconds.
- **Child-Safe & Private**: No child email or password required (join with simple family code). Zero ads, zero tracking.

## 🛠️ How to Update via Script or Console

### Apple App Store Connect:
To re-run automated sync:
```bash
python3 scripts/update_appstore_metadata.py
```
This updates:
1. Live versions: `promotionalText` (active immediately on App Store).
2. Prepared versions: full description, keywords, promotional text, release notes, and attaches the latest eligible build.

### Google Play Console:
- **Option A (Manual Copy-Paste in 30 seconds)**:
  1. Open [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console).
  2. Select **Chores Star** (`com.hoque.familychores`).
  3. Navigate to **Grow** → **Store presence** → **Main store listing**.
  4. Paste the Short Description and Full Description above and click **Save**.
- **Option B (Automated via Script)**:
  1. In Play Console → **Users & permissions** → click the service account (`...gserviceaccount.com`).
  2. Check **"Edit store listing, pricing, and distribution"**.
  3. Run:
     ```bash
     scripts/.play-venv/bin/python scripts/update_playstore_listing.py
     ```
